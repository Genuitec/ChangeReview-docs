# Getting Help

Ask questions, report bugs, and suggest features on the
[ChangeReview Support Forum](https://github.com/Genuitec/ChangeReview-community/discussions).
Your early-access feedback helps shape the production release.

## Reporting a problem

Please include:

- What you expected and what happened.
- Your Eclipse and ChangeReview versions, and operating system.
- The feature you were using: Live Review, Inline Compare, or Time Machine.
- Steps to reproduce the issue and the file's language.
- A screenshot if the issue is visual.

Find version information under **Help > About Eclipse IDE** and
**Installation Details**.

Check the [Changelog](../resources/changelog) for fixes in newer releases.
